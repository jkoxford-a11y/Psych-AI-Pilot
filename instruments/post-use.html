Instrument 3E — Post-Use, Access, and Acceptability Questionnaire (10 items)

How the platform was actually used and experienced · pilot-section students only, end of semester

  1. How often did you use the college-provided AI platform during this course? — A. Never. B. Once or twice. C. Daily. D. Weekly. E. Monthly.
  2. For which course-related purposes did you use it? (open field)
  3. When you used AI for coursework, which platform did you use most often? — A. College-provided platform. B. Another free tool. C. Another paid tool. D. Did not use AI.
  4. Did the college-provided account give you access to capabilities you otherwise would not have had? — A. Yes. B. No. C. Unsure.
  5. Did you reduce or cancel a personal paid AI subscription because the college provided access? — A. Yes. B. No. C. Not applicable.
  6. What barriers limited your use? (select all that apply) — no need; difficult interface; technical problem; privacy concern; course policy uncertainty; preference for another tool; accessibility issue; other.
  7. The platform helped me complete course learning activities more effectively. (1 = strongly disagree … 5 = strongly agree)
  8. The AI-literacy activities improved my ability to evaluate an AI answer about psychology. (1–5)
  9. I would use the college-provided platform in another course if it were available. (1–5)
  10. What is one benefit, limitation, or change that should inform future use of AI in courses? (open response)
