The Analogy Stress Test

proposed

High-ROI Assignment Bank · Deep Conceptual Processing · Best for neuroscience, physiology, complex mechanisms

Status: proposed, not yet adopted for the pilot. Two additional workspace copies of this assignment bank exist in the source Notion with unresolved duplicate status against this original.

The Concept

AI generates convincing but imperfect analogies. Students must find the biological or theoretical flaws.

The Prompt

"Ask the AI to explain [Concept X] using an analogy involving [Everyday Topic Y] (e.g., explain action potentials using a toilet flush)."

Student Deliverable

  • The Transcript: the AI's analogy.
  • The Audit: a "Limitations Analysis" (150 words) identifying exactly where the analogy breaks down.
    Example: "The toilet flush works for the all-or-none principle, but fails to explain the relative refractory period because a toilet tank refills at a constant rate, unlike ion channels which have specific voltage-gated resetting states."

Why It Works

You can't critique an analogy unless you understand the science perfectly.

Grading ROI

High. You only read the "Limitations" paragraph.

Implementation Rubric — "AI Auditor" Assignments

The source page proposes one standardized rubric across all six High-ROI assignments:

CriteriaPass (100%)Revise (0%)
The PromptPrompt is included and follows the specific structure (e.g., "Act as a patient…").Prompt is missing or generic ("Tell me about X").

The FormattingClearly labels "Student Work" vs. "AI Output."Hard to distinguish student voice from AI text.

Instructor note from the source: "Revise" does not mean fail — it means do it again until you actually critique the AI. This maintains high standards without creating a grading backlog.
