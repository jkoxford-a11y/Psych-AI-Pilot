The "Simulated Patient" Interview

proposed

High-ROI Assignment Bank · Inquiry and Application · Best for abnormal psychology, diagnostics, clinical interviewing

Status: proposed, not yet adopted for the pilot. Two additional workspace copies of this assignment bank exist in the source Notion with unresolved duplicate status against this original. The simulated patient is entirely AI-generated fiction — no real client, patient, or case data should ever be used here.

The Concept

Turns static case studies into dynamic, responsive interviews.

The Prompt

The student pastes a system prompt into the AI: "You are a patient named Alex. You have symptoms of [Disorder X] but are trying to hide them / are in denial. Answer my questions evasively. Do not reveal your diagnosis."

Student Deliverable

  • The Transcript: 10–15 minutes of the student "interviewing" the AI.
  • The Diagnosis: a "Diagnostic Rationale" citing the specific behavioral evidence from the chat that matches DSM criteria.

Why It Works

Forces active inquiry and evidence-gathering rather than just recognizing a definition.

Grading ROI

Medium. You ignore the transcript and check the "Rationale" for evidence.

Implementation Rubric — "AI Auditor" Assignments

The source page proposes one standardized rubric across all six High-ROI assignments:

CriteriaPass (100%)Revise (0%)
The PromptPrompt is included and follows the specific structure (e.g., "Act as a patient…").Prompt is missing or generic ("Tell me about X").
The AuditCritique/reflection identifies specific flaws, limits, or missing info. Cites course concepts.Audit is a summary ("The AI did a good job") rather than a critique. No specific evidence cited.
The FormattingClearly labels "Student Work" vs. "AI Output."Hard to distinguish student voice from AI text.

Instructor note from the source: "Revise" does not mean fail — it means do it again until you actually critique the AI. This maintains high standards without creating a grading backlog.
