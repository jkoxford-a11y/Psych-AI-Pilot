Glossary

 

MAILS
Meta AI Literacy Scale — a published self-report instrument measuring AI literacy, self-efficacy, and self-management
GLAT
A published, performance-based instrument for measuring generative-AI literacy directly, rather than by self-report
PHS
The institution's human-subjects research review process (IRB-equivalent) — not required for this pilot's internal academic-assessment use, only if results are later published or presented
Literacy tier
The first of four developmental tiers: basic mental model of what AI is and isn't, disclosure of use, and catching obvious errors
Fluency tier
Deliberate, documented, iterative use of AI with verification against course sources
Proficiency tier
Discipline-specific auditing of AI output using psychological theory, research, and ethical standards
Mastery tier
Strategic orchestration of AI use with full, defensible accountability for every consequential decision
Protected assessment
An assessment genuinely resistant to AI assistance, used to verify what a student can actually do without AI support
AI Use and Evaluation Statement
A structured disclosure students complete describing how they used AI, what they verified, and what they contributed themselves
BoodleBox
The institutional AI platform used across the pilot
TILT
Transparency in Learning and Teaching — the format model the Assignment Bank's turnkey packets follow
