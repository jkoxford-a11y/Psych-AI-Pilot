Student AI Toolkit & Guide (external site)

What you actually hand to students. A four-stop guide, in order: About the pilot and what's different about the course, Getting Started with BoodleBox (building the account and first bot), Environment (what AI use actually costs, in plain numbers), and the self-guided Toolkit that walks them through the fundamental competencies — context, verification, model differences, responsible use — with experiments and a Check-In they submit. Student-facing only; it deliberately never links back to this site.
