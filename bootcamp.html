Faculty Bootcamp

A 6-module onboarding sequence, designed to be walked through in a single session including hands-on time. You don't need to come in as an expert — by the end, you'll have a working BoodleBox bot loaded with your course context. Live at jkoxford-a11y.github.io/psych_bootcamp/bootcamp/. complete

0. Why This Matters

If it's online and can be clicked, AI can do it — including your assignments. Introduces agentic AI browsing before anything else, so the rest of the bootcamp has real stakes.

1. Getting Started

You don't need to prepare, you need to start — the only way to develop a feel for this is to use it on something real.

2. How AI Actually Works (Enough to Matter)

AI has working memory, not unlimited memory. Long chats degrade — a few habits make this a non-issue.

3. Getting Useful, Honest Output

Vague prompts produce generic responses, and AI defaults to agreement. Both problems share the same fix: be specific about what you want and how you want it delivered.

4. BoodleBox: Building Your Course Workspace

The institutional AI tool for this pilot. You build a bot loaded with your course materials during this module — everything from Module 3 applies directly.

5. Verifying What You Get Back

AI sounds authoritative whether it's right or wrong — tone isn't a reliability signal. A few habits protect you from confidently using something incorrect.

6. What's Next

The bootcamp was the floor. Literacy develops through use, not more reading.

AI Course Projects and Custom Bots

30–45 min

A guided build activity: construct one reusable AI workspace or custom chatbot for a course or recurring teaching task, test it, revise it, and document what you made. Not about mastering every platform — about shipping one working thing.
