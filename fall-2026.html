Fall 2026 Pilot

The current, decided scope for the active pilot — distinct from the Four-Year Roadmap, which is still a proposal.

Fall-only Literacy tier All courses with psych majors No outcome claims yet

Scope

  • Fall-only. No Spring 2027 data collection under this pilot cycle. The four-year vision stays a roadmap to revisit once this pilot's evidence is reviewed.
  • Literacy tier only. A one-semester pilot can responsibly target the first developmental tier — conceptual understanding, limitations, disclosure, catching obvious errors — not the higher tiers.
  • Every course with enrolled psychology majors participates — not a single designated pilot course.
  • PSYC 101 (Introductory/General Psychology) remains the intended long-term entry point, but isn't hosting activities this Fall (instructor capacity). That rollout is deferred to Spring 2027 or later.

Proposed course activities (draft, under faculty review)

Originally drafted as priority tiers before the "every course with psych majors" scope decision, so this is a starting proposal, not the final roster.

Deep integration

PSYC 224 — Statistics

Fluency

AI Statistical Consultant Audit. Students determine whether an AI-generated interpretation of a research scenario is statistically appropriate — correct test, p-value interpretation, effect size, assumptions, causal overclaiming.

PSYC 225 — Quantitative Research Methods

Fluency

From Research Question to AI-Audited Design. AI generates hypotheses/variables/designs from a human-written research question; students audit for construct/internal/external validity, confounds, sampling, feasibility, ethics.

PSYC 275W — Intro to Social Science Writing

Literacy → Fluency

Revision With Accountability. Human draft → AI critique → student critique of the critique → revised draft → revision memo.

PSYC 220 — Cognitive Psychology

Fluency

AI Is Not Human Memory. Compare human cognition to AI output generation — semantic memory vs. pattern completion, source monitoring vs. hallucination, framing effects across prompt variants.

Selective integration

PSYC 250 — Psychological Disorders

Literacy → Fluency

DSM Criteria vs. Narrative Seduction. Evaluate whether an AI-generated case conceptualization is actually justified by the criteria.

PSYC 336 — Industrial/Organizational Psychology

Fluency

AI for job analysis, performance-appraisal bias simulation, AI as HR assistant — where it helps, where it fails, legal risk.

PSYC 330 — Lifespan Development

Literacy

Generate developmental case studies across age cohorts; digital literacy and aging as course content.

Targeted moments

PSYC 381 — History and Systems

Audit AI summaries of historical figures/theories for accuracy and bias — hallucinations as a lesson in source reliability.

PSYC 210 — Clinical Helping Skills

Empathy Is Not Just Nice Language. Compare AI-generated "supportive" responses with helping-skills responses grounded in course principles; rewrite using real principles.

If your course isn't listed above but has psychology majors enrolled this Fall, it's still in scope — see Activities & Assignments for a browsable bank to build your own.

What we're measuring

The pilot's specific, bounded claim this Fall is a measurable pre/post gain in AI literacy among participating students — see Assessment Approach.

What we're building toward

Each participating course also contributes at least one portfolio artifact — a lightweight record of what AI did, how the student checked or revised it, and what psychological judgment they contributed. The first building block of the longer-term portfolio vision in the Roadmap.

This is a first-year pilot generating evidence, not proof that the approach already improves outcomes. Claims about this pilot stay proportional to the data actually collected.
